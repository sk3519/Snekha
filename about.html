It started at a pond in Rajnagar.


In 2019, our founder watched a farmer sell a full day's makhana harvest to a middleman for less than what one retail pack sells for in Delhi. That math never made sense — so we decided to fix it.

We began buying directly from four families. Today we partner with 120+ farming households across six districts of the Mithila belt, paying 20–30% above mandi rates and settling within 48 hours. On your side of the bag, that means makhana that is roasted — never fried — with labels you can actually read aloud.

  • 2020 — First direct purchase: 400 kg from 4 families in Rajnagar, Madhubani.
  • 2022 — Grading & roasting facility set up in Madhubani town.
  • 2024 — 100th farming family onboarded; B2B supply begins.
  • 2026 — 6 flavoured SKUs, natural range, nuts & spices. Retail + bulk across India.
